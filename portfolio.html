About Me

I'm a Digital Marketing specialist working across analytics, AI-driven search, and social strategy, currently completing an MSc in Digital & Data Marketing at NEOMA Business School, and interning at Lallemand Brewing in Toulouse.

I like turning ambiguous problems into measurable systems: tracking architectures that tell the truth about user behavior, AI-search strategies that keep content discoverable as search itself changes, and social content that actually reaches people. That instinct still shapes how I work today.
