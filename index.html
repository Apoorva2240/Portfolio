My story

I'm a Digital Marketing specialist working across analytics, AI-driven search, and social strategy, currently completing an MSc in Digital & Data Marketing at NEOMA Business School, and interning at Lallemand Brewing in Toulouse.

I like turning ambiguous problems into measurable systems: tracking architectures that tell the truth about user behavior, AI-search strategies that keep content discoverable as search itself changes, and social content that actually reaches people. My path here started somewhere unexpected — multiple roles in luxury hospitality between 2019 and 2023, at brands like The Leela Palace, Taj Mahal Palace and Towers, and MEGU — where I learned that every detail is a brand decision. That instinct still shapes how I work today.

Outside of work: football, new trails, and whatever game the internet won't stop talking about.
